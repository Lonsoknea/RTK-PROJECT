YEAK LAOM LAKE

A Natural Wonder in the Heart of Ratanakiri.


Hidden among the lush forests of northeastern Cambodia, discover a remarkable volcanic lake where nature, culture, and history come together.



Located approximately five kilometers from Banlung, the capital of Ratanakiri Province, Yeak Laom Lake is one of Cambodia's most remarkable natural destinations.Formed by ancient volcanic activity, this nearly circular lake is surrounded by dense forests and offers a peaceful natural escape. Its deep, clear waters and distinctive landscape attract visitors who wish to experience the beauty of Cambodia's northeastern region.However, Yeak Laom is more than just a scenic destination. It is also a place of cultural and spiritual importance to the Tampuan Indigenous community, whose generations of connection with the surrounding land have helped shape the identity of this remarkable place.
Beyond its natural beauty, Yeak Laom offers visitors an opportunity to slow down and enjoy a closer connection with nature. The surrounding area provides a peaceful setting for walking, sightseeing, photography, and spending time with family and friends. A walking trail around the lake allows visitors to explore different viewpoints while appreciating the forest and the scenery from different angles.The lake is also a wonderful destination for those who enjoy outdoor activities. Visitors can relax near the water, admire the landscape, or experience swimming in designated areas where permitted. Whether someone comes for adventure, relaxation, or simply to appreciate the scenery, Yeak Laom offers an experience that feels different from the busy atmosphere of city life.

What makes this destination especially meaningful is the relationship between its natural environment and the people who have lived alongside it for generations. The lake represents more than a place to visit; it reflects the connection between nature, cultural identity, and local heritage.For travelers exploring Ratanakiri, Yeak Laom is an opportunity to discover a different side of Cambodia — one where remarkable landscapes and living traditions exist together. It invites visitors not only to admire its beauty but also to understand the stories and values that make this place truly special.


Every visit offers a chance to appreciate the beauty of the natural world and the importance of protecting it. By respecting local traditions and keeping the environment clean, visitors can help preserve this special destination for future generations. Yeak Laom remains a place where nature, culture, and unforgettable experiences come together.

Whether visitors come to explore the forest, enjoy the peaceful atmosphere, learn about local culture, or simply escape from the busy routine of everyday life, Yeak Laom offers something meaningful for everyone. Its unique combination of natural beauty and cultural heritage makes it more than just a tourist attraction; it is a place where visitors can create lasting memories while developing a deeper appreciation for Cambodia's natural treasures. As one of Ratanakiri's remarkable destinations, Yeak Laom continues to welcome travelers seeking a refreshing and memorable experience.



If you are looking for a destination where you can relax, explore nature, and experience local culture, Yeak Laom Lake is definitely worth adding to your travel list. It is an ideal place for families, friends, couples, and nature lovers who want to enjoy a refreshing escape from busy city life. Visitors are encouraged to bring their cameras to capture the beautiful scenery, explore the walking trails, enjoy the fresh air, and learn more about the traditions of the local Bunong community. For the best experience, consider visiting during the dry season when the weather is generally more suitable for outdoor activities. Remember to respect local customs, follow safety guidelines, and keep the area clean throughout your visit.

Don't just hear about the beauty of Yeak Laom—come and experience it yourself! Discover its peaceful atmosphere, admire its natural wonders, and create unforgettable memories at one of Cambodia's most beautiful natural destinations.
