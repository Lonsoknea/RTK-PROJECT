Ratanakiri

Beyond the Ordinary,Into the Wild.

Ratanakiri is one of the most beautiful provinces in northeastern Cambodia, known for its breathtaking landscapes, lush green forests, stunning waterfalls, and rich cultural heritage. From peaceful natural scenery to unique local traditions, the province offers countless places waiting to be explored. Among its many remarkable destinations, Yeak Laom Lake stands out as one of its most fascinating natural wonders.

Located near Banlung, the provincial capital,

Yeak Laom Lake

is a beautiful volcanic crater lake surrounded by dense green forest. Its crystal-clear water, peaceful atmosphere, and unique natural landscape make it a must-visit destination for anyone exploring Ratanakiri. More than just a place of natural beauty, the lake also holds cultural and spiritual significance for the local Tampuan Indigenous community. Join us as we discover the beauty of Ratanakiri and explore the stories, nature, and cultural heritage surrounding Yeak Laom Lake.
